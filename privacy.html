Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Diampro 360 ("Diampro", "the app"), developed by Gopal Vastarpara, handles information, including the information it receives from Google when a business connects Google Drive.

Where your data is kept

Diampro 360 is installed and run by each business itself: a desktop application on the business's computers and a server on the business's own network. The business data entered in the app (kapans, job work, parties, payments and similar records) is stored on that server, under the business's control. The developer does not run a central server that collects this data, and does not receive copies of it.

Google user data the app accesses

Connecting Google Drive is optional. An administrator of the business starts it in the app and signs in with the Google account that should hold the exports. With the business's permission, Diampro 360 accesses only:

  • The email address of the connected Google account, to show the business which account exports are saved to.
  • Files and folders the app itself creates in that Google Drive, through Google's drive.file permission: a "Diampro Exports" folder and the CSV export files placed in it. The app cannot see, open or change any other files in the Drive.

How the app uses Google user data

  • It creates the "Diampro Exports" folder when Drive is connected.
  • It uploads an export file to that folder each time a user of the business asks for an export.
  • It shows the connected account's email address and a link to the folder in the app.

Google user data is not used for any other purpose.

Storage and security

When Drive is connected, Google issues tokens that let the app upload on the business's behalf. The long-lived token is stored encrypted on the business's own server; short-lived access tokens are kept there for up to an hour. They are used only to upload that business's exports. Export files are created on the business's server, uploaded to Google Drive, and then deleted from the server. Connections to Google use HTTPS.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with or transferred to any third party. It is not sent to the developer. Who can open the exported files in Google Drive is decided by the business through Google Drive's own sharing settings.

Limited Use

Diampro 360's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • An administrator can choose Disconnect in the app (Configurations → Google Drive). This asks Google to revoke the app's access and deletes the stored token from the business's server.
  • Access can also be removed at any time from the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Files already exported stay in the business's Google Drive, owned by that account, and can be deleted there like any other file.

Children

Diampro 360 is a business application and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
