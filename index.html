Diampro 360

Business management for diamond processing units, as a desktop application.

Diampro 360 helps diamond businesses run day-to-day work in one place. Each business runs it on its own computers, with its own server on the local network, so the business keeps its data.

What it does

  • Tracks kapans (lots) from assignment to return.
  • Records job work for GLX, Multi LS, Sarin, 4P and factory processing, with stone, weight and labor cost.
  • Manages parties, codes and price charts.
  • Records payments to karigars and from buyers, linked to the work they cover.
  • Shows dashboards and exports lists as CSV reports.

Google Drive

A business can connect its own Google account so that the reports it exports are saved to a "Diampro Exports" folder in its Google Drive, with a sub-folder for each list. Diampro 360 only asks for access to the files it creates there, and the business can disconnect at any time. Read the Privacy Policy for exactly what is used and how.
